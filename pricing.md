# Pricing: SageThumbs 2K

Last updated: 2026-11-06. Machine-readable pricing for agentic buyers and comparison
tools. Human-readable page: https://sagethumbs.lunarwerx.com/

## Personal / non-commercial

- Price: $0. No account required.
- License: [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://github.com/LunarWerxs/SageThumbs-2k/blob/main/.github/LICENSE.md)
- Source: source-available on GitHub, https://github.com/LunarWerxs/SageThumbs-2k
- Included free: thumbnails for all 364 supported formats, Explorer's preview pane, the
  right-click menu (convert, resize and the rest) except its OCR items, the command line,
  and every update.
- Platform: Windows 11 or Windows 10, 64-bit only.

### Supporter (home use)

- Price: US$2 a month or US$20 a year. Buy: yearly https://go.lunarwerx.com/sagethumbs/support ; monthly https://go.lunarwerx.com/sagethumbs/support/monthly
- Covers five features: Quick preview (the Space-bar viewer), the screenshot tool, text
  recognition (OCR, including the right-click OCR items and searchable PDF), settings sync
  across PCs, and the tools AI assistants use (the MCP server).
- Each of the five is free for 14 days from the first time you use it, with no card and no sign-up.

## Commercial / business use

Two plans, same price unit, and every feature included (Supporter's five too). The only differences are how
often you pay and what happens when you stop. Tax is added at checkout where it applies.

- Licence unit (both billing periods): installation. Buy one per PC; set the quantity at checkout.
  One redemption key (`esk_...`) per installation is emailed on payment. Redeem under
  Settings > Licence (version 3.0 and later). Card payment via Stripe.

### Subscription (Business)

- Price: US$50 per installation per year (two months free), or US$5 per installation per month.
- How to buy: yearly https://go.lunarwerx.com/sagethumbs/buy ; monthly https://go.lunarwerx.com/sagethumbs/buy/monthly
- Cancellation: any time, self-serve, at https://go.lunarwerx.com/sagethumbs/manage - sign in
  with the email used to pay. The licence runs to the end of the period already paid for
  and then stops entitling the machine; nothing is pro-rated.
- What it gives: commercial (work) use, priority support, every Supporter feature and every paid feature added from now on.
- Free home use keeps updating as before.

### Volume

- Volume, site licences, purchase orders or bank transfer (roughly 25+ PCs): request a
  quote with the form at https://sagethumbs.lunarwerx.com/#pricing (Request a quote)

## Ongoing costs the user actually bears

- $0 beyond the plan itself. For business, the US$50 a year (or US$5 a month) per installation IS the
  whole recurring cost; for home, it is $0 or the optional Supporter US$2 a month or US$20 a year.
  There is no bundled or required third-party API. Screen OCR uses Windows' own
  in-box recognizer, not a cloud OCR service or an API key. Thumbnailing, conversion,
  and the toolkit are 100% local processing, with no per-use cost.
- The only feature that touches the network at all is the optional right-click
  "Upload and copy link" tool, which posts to a free, keyless image host chosen by
  SageThumbs 2K; it requires no account and no API key from the user.

## What is NOT charged for

- No per-format fee, no watermarking on the free tier, no usage cap, no seat limit
  for personal use. The five Supporter features need no card or sign-up for their 14 free days.
